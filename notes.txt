Ever since the human genome was published, the challenge became to understand how genomes are regulated to allow for the diversity and functions of the cells.

Only a small part of genomes codes proteins. 

Gene expression involves multiple complex processes. Major players in regulation are transcription factor proteins.

Transcription processes are also affected by epigenetic mechanisms. These include DNA methylation, microRNAs and histone tail modifications.
