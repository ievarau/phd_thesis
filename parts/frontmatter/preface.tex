\begin{preface}

This thesis is submitted in partial fulfilment of the requirements for the degree of \textit{Philosophiae Doctor} at the University of Oslo. The presented work was conducted at the Centre for Molecular Medicine Norway (NCMM), organized by the University of Oslo, under the supervision of Professor Dr. Anthony Mathelier and co-supervision of Professor Dr. Jonas Paulsen. This research was funded by the XXXXXXXXXX.

A collection of three articles is the basis of this thesis. Two of the papers are published and one is in peer-review at the date of the submission of the thesis. The overarching theme of presented articles is gene regulation by TF-DNA interactions. Introductory chapters are provided to give necessary theoretical overview and motivation for this research.
