\begin{preface}

This thesis is submitted in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of \textit{Philosophiae Doctor} at the University of Oslo. The presented work was conducted at the Centre for Molecular Medicine Norway (NCMM), organized by the University of Oslo, under the supervision of Professor Dr. Anthony Mathelier and the co-supervision of Associate Professor Dr. Jonas Paulsen. The Research Council of Norway funded this research through grants 187615 and 288404, the Norwegian Cancer Society through grant 197884, and the Nordic EMBL Partnership Hub for Molecular Medicine through NordForsk grant 96782.

A collection of three articles is the basis of this thesis. Two papers were published, and one is in peer review at the thesis submission date. The overarching theme of the presented articles is transcription factor binding to the DNA. Introductory chapters provide the necessary theoretical overview and motivation for this research.
